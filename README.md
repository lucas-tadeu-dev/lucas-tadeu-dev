## 👨‍💻 **About me**

<br>

I am a Backend Developer with over 7 years of experience building scalable and high-impact solutions.

I have strong expertise in Java, AWS, and SQL, with experience designing and developing robust APIs, optimizing database performance, and implementing efficient backend architectures. I also work with CI/CD pipelines to ensure fast, reliable, and automated deployments.

In my projects, I designed and developed a system managing over 10,000 policies and impacting more than 500 agencies, improving operational efficiency and supporting scalable business growth. I have also contributed to backend systems that enhanced performance, increased reliability, and enabled cloud-based scalability. I am fluent in English and open to global opportunities, especially in high-performance environments where I can apply my expertise in Java, AWS, and modern backend architectures.
